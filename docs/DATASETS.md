# HyperDock-Bench V1 datasets

Twelve synthetic instances in `datasets/hyperdock_bench/HB-V1-<scenario>-<N>.json`,
generated by `core/benchmarks/generator.py` (`HyperDockBenchGenerator`) through
`experiments/regenerate_datasets.py`. Regenerating with the committed code reproduces
all twelve committed files exactly (checked by comparing the parsed JSON).

## Generation

- **Seed.** Each instance has a deterministic seed: the MD5 hash of its benchmark ID
  (for example `HB-V1-SC1-50`) modulo 2³², passed to `numpy.random.default_rng`.
- **Item dimensions (w, d, h in m).** Drawn from cargo-transfer-bag style sizes:
  SINGLE 0.502 × 0.425 × 0.248 (p = 0.4), HALF 0.502 × 0.425 × 0.124 (0.3),
  QUARTER 0.251 × 0.425 × 0.124 (0.1), DOUBLE 0.502 × 0.425 × 0.496 (0.1), and
  IRREGULAR, with each side uniform on [0.1, 0.8] (0.1).
- **Category and mass.** Category probabilities (consumable, hardware, payload, safety):
  SC1 and SC3 (0.5, 0.3, 0.1, 0.1); SC2 (0.2, 0.3, 0.1, 0.4). Mass is lognormal by
  category, with (μ, σ) of (1.5, 0.6) for consumable, (2.2, 0.8) hardware, (1.8, 0.5)
  payload and (1.0, 0.4) safety, clipped to [0.1, 80] kg.
- **Other fields.** `priority` is uniform on the integers 1–100 (used by BFD-P only).
  `criticality` and `retrieval_lambda` are set per category and are not used by the
  objectives. `preferred_zone` is always GENERIC.
- **Container.** One container whose volume is 1.25 × the total item volume for SC1 and
  SC2 (utilisation 0.80) and 1.04 × for SC3 (utilisation 0.96). Width and depth are
  max(1, V^(1/3)) and height is V / (w d), so every container is a cube of side
  1.37–3.17 m. Dimensions are rounded to 0.1 mm.
- **Unused metadata.** The container's `open_face_normal` (0, −1, 0) and
  `absolute_origin_xyz_meters` are not used by the decoder or the objectives. The
  CASC model measures access depth along z from the z = 0 face (Section 3.4 of the
  manuscript).

## Known limitations

- Utilisation is a volume ratio only. Whether a complete packing exists is not
  guaranteed. No method in this study (NSGA-II, the variant, random search, FF,
  BFD-V, BFD-P) found one on any SC3 instance.
- Items are rigid cuboids. The irregular class is still a cuboid with random sides.
- Masses and category mixes are illustrative, not taken from a mission manifest.
